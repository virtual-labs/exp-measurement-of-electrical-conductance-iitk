<b>Theory of experiment</b>
<br>
Conductance is the reciprocal of electrical resistance. Unit for conductance is mho or siemens (S) [1 S = 1 ohm<sup>-1</sup>]. Specific conductivity (K) is defined conductance of solution of 1 cm length and having 1 cm<sup>2</sup> area or conductance of 1 cm<sup>3</sup> of the solution of electrolyte.  <br>

Specific conductance = Measured conductance (ohm<sup>-1</sup>) × Cell constant (cm<sup>-1</sup>) -------------------- eq (i) <br>

Equivalent conductance at dilution V cm<sup>3</sup> is defined as the conductance of all ions produced from one-gram equivalent electrolyte dissolved in V cm<sup>3</sup> when the distance between the electrodes is one and electrodes are so large that the whole solution is contained between them. Molar conductivity of solution at dilution V cm<sup>3</sup>, is the conductance of all the ions from one mole of electrolyte dissolved in V cm<sup>3</sup> when the distance between the electrodes is one and electrodes are so large that the whole solution is contained between them. <br>

ᴧ<sub>m</sub> = K × 1000/Molarity      -------------------- eq (ii)    <br>                                                                                                 
ᴧ<sub>eq</sub>= K × 1000/Normality    -------------------- eq (iii)    <br>

Degree of dissociation (α) is the ratio of equivalent conductance (ᴧeq) to the conductance at infinite dilution <br>

 (ᴧ<sub>∞</sub>). (α)= (ᴧ<sub>eq</sub>)/ (ᴧ.∞).    -------------------- eq (iv)                         <br>                                                                                          
 ᴧ<sub>∞</sub> = V<sub>A</sub> ᴧ<sub>∞</sub><sup>+</sup> +V<sub>B</sub> ᴧ<sub>∞</sub><sup>-</sup> (Kohlrausch’s law).      -------------------- eq (v)          <br>                                                                
Where V<sub>A</sub> and V<sub>B</sub> is the number of ions dissociated from molecule cation and anion respectively. <br>

Dissociation constant (K<sub>d</sub>) for acetic acid could be given by the following formula <br>

K<sub>d</sub>=Cα<sup>2</sup>/(1-α).          -------------------- eq (vi)                      <br>
